DMCA Policy

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

Important Notice

VideoSaver Pro does not host any video content. We are a technical tool that processes publicly available URLs from third-party platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, etc.). All videos remain on their original platforms.

How Our Service Works

  • Users provide a URL from a supported platform
  • We extract the direct video link from that platform
  • The video downloads directly from the original platform to the user's device
  • We do not store, cache, or redistribute any video content

If Your Content Is Being Infringed

Since we don't host videos, please take the following steps:

  1. Contact the original platform (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, etc.) to remove the infringing content
  2. Once removed from the source, our service will no longer be able to process that URL
  3. If you believe our service is being used to facilitate infringement, contact us

DMCA Takedown Request

If you are a copyright owner and believe our service facilitates infringement, you may submit a DMCA notice with:

  • Your physical or electronic signature
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
  • The specific URL on the original platform containing your content
  • Your contact information (name, address, phone, email)
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief the use is not authorized
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf

Submit DMCA Notice

Send your DMCA notice through our Contact Page with the subject line "DMCA Takedown Request".

Our Response

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:

  • Review the request within 48 hours
  • Block the specific URL if appropriate
  • Respond to the complainant with our decision

Counter-Notification

If you believe content was wrongly blocked, you may submit a counter-notification with the information required under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3).
