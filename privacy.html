Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

VideoSaver Pro is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Video URLs: Temporarily processed to fetch download links. Not stored.
  • IP Addresses: Used only for rate limiting to prevent abuse. Not logged permanently.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand usage patterns (anonymized).

2. What We Don't Collect

  • We do NOT require registration or login
  • We do NOT store your downloaded videos
  • We do NOT track your browsing history
  • We do NOT sell any user data

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare: Hosting and CDN services
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics
  • RapidAPI: Video processing API

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies required for:

  • Google Analytics (can be blocked with browser settings)
  • No advertising or tracking cookies

5. Data Security

We protect your data through:

  • HTTPS encryption on all connections
  • No permanent storage of personal data
  • Cloudflare security protection

6. Your Rights

Since we don't store personal data, there's nothing to delete. However, you can:

  • Block cookies in your browser settings
  • Use browser extensions to block Google Analytics
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

7. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at: Contact Page

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Check the "Last Updated" date above for the latest version.
